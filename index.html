Duel Board Builder

Build a Yu-Gi-Oh! duel board for your AO3 fic by clicking and typing. No code to write: copy the result into your chapter.

1 Install the Work Skin (once)

  1. On AO3, go to Dashboard → Skins → My Work Skins → Create Work Skin.
  2. Give it any title, paste the Work Skin into the CSS box, and submit.
  3. On your fic's Edit page, pick it under Select Work Skin and post.

2 Edit a zone

Duelists and turn

Damage line (optional)

A banner for attacks and LP changes, to put in the story between boards.

Live preview

Click any zone, or any card in a hand, to edit it. Point at cards to try the pop-ups and info panels, just like readers will.

3 Copy into your chapter

  1. In your chapter's editor on AO3, switch to HTML (not Rich Text).
  2. Paste the board where you want it in the story, then preview or post.
Edit a board you made before

Paste a board's code here (for example, copied back out of your chapter's HTML) to keep editing it, such as for the next turn.

Your board is saved in this browser as you work. Card details come from YGOPRODeck's free card database.
